Featured project · NVIDIA Research · 2026

SoL-Pi

Scaling Auto-Research Loops for Efficient Agent Harnesses

Core project lead · Co-first author

Technical blog ↗ GitHub ↗

Nearly 1K GitHub stars within three days of public release.

I co-led the automated research workflow behind SoL-Pi. Starting from 152 proposed directions, agents autonomously implemented, tested, and refined candidate mechanisms. Four survived validation to form an efficient coding-agent harness. My work spanned research priors, workflow design and implementation, and refinement of the surviving code for release.

On EdgeBench, SoL-Pi used 45–49% fewer tokens and about one-third lower API-equivalent cost than Pi, retaining approximately 94% of Pi’s average score across GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.

I also extended SoL-Pi to multi-agent search with one coordinator and 20 workers. In a two-hour kernel-optimization comparison, the SoL-Pi swarm reached 17.5% fewer simulated cycles at 26.8% lower API-equivalent cost than the stock-Pi swarm.

Swarm results reflect one run per configuration. See the technical blog for the evaluation protocol and limitations.
