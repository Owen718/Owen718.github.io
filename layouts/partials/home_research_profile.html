Biography

From generative foundations to useful systems.

I am a PhD Candidate working on Generative AI and Foundation Models. My research builds open systems that push visual generation and restoration forward while remaining efficient, reusable, and ready for real-world deployment.

My research spans foundation models, efficient generative vision, and automated AI systems. I have led and contributed to SANA-Video 2.0, Sol-Attn, LucidFlux, UltraFlux, PosterCraft, PosterOmni, Meissonic, and MagicInfinite.

I am currently a Research Intern at NVIDIA, working on Foundation Models in close collaboration with Dr. Enze Xie, Dr. Haozhe Liu, and Prof. Song Han.
