Automated research

02

Multimodal agents

I contributed to GenEvolve, which turns differences between higher- and lower-quality tool trajectories into visual experience for an image-generation agent.

03

Generative models

I led UltraFlux and LucidFlux, and architected Meissonic, spanning model architecture, data, and efficient image synthesis.

04

Research to production

At Hedra, I co-developed MagicInfinite / Character-3, bringing multimodal generative research into a shipped talking-video product.
