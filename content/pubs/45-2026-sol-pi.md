*Equal contribution. †Core contributors.*

I co-led the automated research workflow behind SoL-Pi. Agents autonomously implement,
test, and refine candidate mechanisms within each search loop; four mechanisms survived
validation and form the released harness. Human contributions include research priors,
workflow design and implementation, and refinement of the surviving code for release.

The [technical blog](https://nvlabs.github.io/SoL-Pi/) describes the search process,
held-out evaluation, and a coordinated search experiment with 20 workers.
